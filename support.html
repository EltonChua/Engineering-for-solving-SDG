Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and we'll send you a link to reset your password. Make sure to check your spam folder if you don't see the email in your inbox.

What are the system requirements for Spryzen products?

System requirements vary by product. Generally, our products require Windows 10 or later, macOS 10.14 or later, or a modern Linux distribution. For specific requirements, please check the product documentation or contact our support team.

How can I update my billing information?

You can update your billing information by logging into your account, navigating to the Billing section, and selecting "Payment Methods". From there, you can add, remove, or update your payment information.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all our products. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Please contact our support team to initiate a refund request.

How do I contact technical support?

You can contact our technical support team through the contact form on this page, by email at support@spryzen.com, or by phone at 1-800-SPRYZEN. Our support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST.
